# CODECO client decisions

**Status** · 21 November 2026. **No decision is pending.** On 21 November 2026 the CODECO project owner instructed the developer to decide every open question on the client's behalf, strictly from the material the client supplied (the workbook `Construction_Project_App_Developer_Specification_FINAL_v2.xlsx`, the Word specification `CODECO_Supervision_App_Developer_Requirements_Menu_and_Contractor_Portal.docx` and images 1–15) and, where that material leaves a choice, in the way that serves the application best. Each decision below is recorded with that instruction as its approval. The six workbook decisions of 20 November (D01–D03, D06–D08) stay in force.
