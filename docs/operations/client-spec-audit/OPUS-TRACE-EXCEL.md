## Real differences, most important first

1. **Inspections are not built as the Excel form.** Type, category, discipline, work activity / linked record, result and drawing reference are required on the sheet, but ours does not ask for them when the inspection is started. They are optional fields that a manager can only edit afterwards in the record view (`InspectionSpecEditor`). The result is the checklist's passed/failed state, not the 6-value Excel result. The Title is optional and hidden. The checklist list is not filtered by type or discipline. Photos are attached per checklist point. There is no sign-off line (`sheetWorkflow` has no entry for inspections).
2. **Works Ongoing 9 "What exactly is being worked on?"** In the Excel this is a set of activity-dependent multi-select buttons taken from Activity Detail Guide column H, which has lists for all 159 activities. Ours is one free-text field, and column H is not in `spec-catalogue.ts`. The related rule "Remarks required when Other" is also absent.
3. **Change Order 16 Approval status** can be edited in the form. Ours is a required select with initial "Draft", and only Approved and Rejected are reviewer-only, so a user can set Submitted, Returned, Withdrawn, On Hold, Implemented or Closed by hand. The Excel makes it a read-only status that the PM approval action sets.
4. **HSE 13 Risk rating** is not calculated as likelihood × severity unless the project has configured and enabled a matrix. Without one, the user picks Low, Medium, High or Critical by hand. The Excel says it is always system-calculated and read-only.
5. **HSE Incident 19 Potential / actual severity uses the wrong scale.** Ours uses "Insignificant" to "Catastrophic". The Excel uses "Level 1 – Minor" to "Level 5 – Critical" (Risk & Severity Scales R22–26). The suggestion from the injury, damage or environment answers is missing, and so is the default "Investigation required = Yes when severity ≥ 3".
6. **Location block (Issue, NCR, HSE, Incident, TI, CO, PDR, Photos, Inspections).** The Excel block is: pick a drawing, place a pin, building and level fill automatically, and the detail text comes from the pin. Ours has building and location-register pickers (plus a GPS map on Issue and HSE). Only Works Ongoing and Works Completed have the drawing picker, and even there the building does not come from the drawing and the pin does not fill "Exact location". Pre-fills that depend on this block are also absent (TI 8 and CO 11 drawing reference, Inspections 9).
7. **Workforce has no database record ID.** `v5_form_code` has no WF branch, so <PROJECT>-WF-<YYYY>-<NNNN> is never assigned. The form also has no Title and no Remarks (only a note under "Deviating workforce"), and uses the labels "Party", "Trade" and "Counted by". The row order is Party | Trade, with a counter instead of a number input.
8. **Photos.** There is no per-photo Title and no sign-off profile (PHOTO-LOG-SUPERVISION is missing). Purpose is a dropdown, not click buttons. Related record cannot be picked in the photo dialog, so it cannot pre-fill discipline and location. The date/time is an editable field defaulting to the file's modified time, where the Excel shows it read-only in the auto bar with an EXIF default.
9. **Materials Delivery has no purchase-order register.** Supplier, material group and category, description, ordered quantity and unit should fill automatically from the PO and be read-only. Ours enters them all by hand. Delivery status is not pre-selected automatically. Received by is an editable, required picker in the form rather than the read-only auto bar user.
10. **Contractor "resubmit" stage** (Issue, HSE, NCR stage 4 in `signoff.ts`). Our profiles give the Contractor the "resubmit" action. Sign-off sheet R5 allows a contractor only Acknowledge, Respond, Upload evidence and Witness.
11. **Event date shown twice** (Issue, NCR, HSE, WO, WC, TI, CO, LL, Agreement, Delivery). The Excel puts the date/time in the read-only auto bar. Ours shows "now" in the auto bar and the editable date again as a field in the form body. PDR, Incident and Agreement also lack the "default = now" on their date.
12. **Other missing link-first fills and defaults:**
    - NCR fields 8, 10, 11, 19 from the related inspection.
    - NCR related inspection is not near the top.
    - HSE action owner from person involved.
    - TI action owner from the issued-to company.
    - PDR action owner from the responsible party.
    - PDR affected activity has no discipline/activity dropdowns and does not fill the location.
    - WO shift, unit and crew defaults.
    - WO concrete pre-fill from ready-mix delivery.
    - WC quantity sum and drawing copy.
    - WC status set from the linked inspection (ours only shows the inspection link after "Inspected – …" is chosen).
    - CO originator and currency defaults.
    - Storage location default.
13. **Values that should be read-only are editable.** WC discipline, activity, building, unit and crew (copied from the WO record). Issue discipline when the category fixes it. NCR discipline. TI "Closed / verified by" is an editable picker; the Excel fills it from the sign-off log.
14. **Required rules:**
    - WO Blocker is not required when On Hold, and is hidden until On Hold.
    - Issue 13.1 Responsible person is required in ours (the Excel says optional short text).
    - Issue 13 Responsible party is a role list, not the Project Company Register.
    - Inspections 13 Corrective action is never required.
    - Photos Work location is not required.
15. **Extra fields not in the Excel:**
    - HSE and Incident closure notes, and a required "HSE reviewer" on Incident.
    - PDR "Duration is" and its closure note.
    - CO "Approved by" and "Approval date" in the form.
    - LL "Reviewed by".
    - Delivery planned/revised dates and the "Stored on site" toggle.
    - WO/WC secondary discipline.
    - Inspection "Contractor witness".
    - Workforce visitors, crew, per-building tabs and in-form signature.
16. **List deltas:** CO time unit adds "Hours". Incident notification log parties are free text; the Excel names Arbeidsinspectie – Ministry of Labour, Police, Fire Brigade, NIMOS and Client.
17. **Field order.** `arrange()` reorders every sheet form into the mockup v6 cards, so the order of column A is not kept on any of the 12 sheet forms (one row per form below).
18. **Smaller differences:**
    - Profile codes lack "-SUPERVISION" in `workflowProfiles` and `signoffProfiles`; only `sheetWorkflow` has them.
    - Delivery's workflow line shows "Supplier signs (optional)".
    - CO and Incident stages are numbered 1, 3, 4.
    - Status is shown in the sign-off card rather than the auto bar.
    - Photo button labels differ ("Take photo" / "Upload photos").

Not verified: whether NCR numbers are written back to Inspections field 16, and EXIF reading outside `photo-evidence.tsx`.
